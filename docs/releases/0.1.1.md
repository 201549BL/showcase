Showcase 0.1.1 makes recordings easier to share and brings the editor forward when you finish recording.

## Highlights

- Copy rendered videos to the clipboard directly from Export, using your selected sizes and resolution. Paste into Finder or apps that accept video files without choosing an export folder.
- Stopping a recording brings the editor to the front on your current desktop, including restoring a minimized window.
- Choose Bibata or Capitaine cursors with custom fill and outline colors. New recordings remember your cursor preferences.
- Export multiple sizes in one operation: Desktop, Square, Shorts, and original size, in 1080p or 4K.
- Use image backgrounds and reuse your last background in new recordings.
- Improved camera editing, audio controls, timeline deletion shortcuts, and remembered recording settings.
- Refined floating recorder styling and draggable controls.

## Install

Download `Showcase-0.1.1-universal.zip`, unzip it, and replace Showcase.app in Applications. The app is Developer ID signed and Apple notarized.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. Supports Apple silicon and Intel. Microphone recording requires macOS 15 or newer; native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26.

Existing Showcase and SmoothScreen projects remain supported.
